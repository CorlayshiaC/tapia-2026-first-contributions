# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This repository is a place for people to make their first open source contribution. Many contributors here are doing this for the first time, and everyone deserves to be treated as though their contribution matters — because it does.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient with people who are learning
- Assume good faith
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Respect that people arrive with different backgrounds, tools, and levels of experience

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or dismissive treatment of beginners.

## Reporting

Raise concerns with the repository maintainer. During the Tapia 2026 workshop, speak to Suma Movva or either teaching assistant. Reports will be handled discreetly.

Conduct at the conference itself is also covered by the [ACM Tapia code of conduct](https://tapiaconference.acm.org).
